# Data licence

The files in `public/data/*.json` are derived from:

- **wordhoard** v0.1.0 by natema, https://github.com/natema/wordhoard, CC BY-SA 4.0 (lemmas, part of speech, gender, word forms, CEFR estimates).
- **FrequencyWords** by Hermit Dave, https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords, OpenSubtitles 2018 frequencies, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Changes: filtered to nouns and verbs of levels A1 to C1, reshaped to JSON, ordered by frequency rank (`scripts/build_words.py`).
